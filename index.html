The Grounds

A working mango orchard that happens to have a homestay in it

You arrive down a gravel path under mango canopy, bougainvillea spilling pink and gold overhead, before the yellow bungalow appears through the trees.

The property sits on the Mumbai–Goa highway outside Panvel — close enough for a two-hour drive, far enough that the loudest thing after dark is a guitar on the terrace. Rooms are simple and warm: iron beds, terrazzo floors, rust-red curtains. Everything else — the pool, the orchard, the long dinner table — is built for sitting still.

The Table

Dinner happens outside, under the trees

The centrepiece of Valhalla isn't a room — it's the courtyard table, set among the same mango trees that shade the entrance, wrapped in fairy lights after sundown.

Indoors, the lounge keeps the same palette: mustard walls, deep-green wainscoting, a long communal table for the nights it rains. A chalkboard by the door lists whatever's on for the evening — usually live music, always good food.
